   finger collision meshes `dex1_col_{1,2}.stl`. Measured: finger joint -0.020 = closed (5.8 mm pad gap), +0.0245 = open

## Kinematics findings (the hard part)
- Kitchen counters are 0.92 m; the G1's shoulder is 1.08 m (pelvis 0.79 m, straight legs). Arms are short (TCP ~0.55 m
  from the shoulder with the 115 mm Dex1). Reaching **in front of the chest** at counter height drives the elbow into the
  counter edge or the chest, so each arm works **outboard of its own shoulder** (robot frame |y| 0.12-0.30 m, x 0.27-0.46 m).
- URDF collision meshes are convex hulls; the torso hull is fatter than the real chest and blocked normal reaching
  (shoulder motors saturating, hand lagging 15-25 cm). Excluded torso <-> upper arm/elbow contacts; the planner instead
  keeps the elbow >= 10 cm from the body centre line.
- Grasp: side grasp, gripper pitched down (search 0/15/30 deg), approach yaw searched (cups are round), TCP at 72 % of
  cup height (keeps the palm's top-front corner above the rim and fingertips clear of the plate). Vertical approach,
  vertical lift, vertical place, vertical retreat (sideways retreats knocked cups over).
- Physics: pads condim 6, friction (1.5, 0.08, 0.004) - torsional friction stops cups pivoting in the grasp (cups were
  being released tilted and toppling). Gripper 40 N.

## For F2 (data notes)
- Format as agreed; extras: target_pose (T,7), tcp_active (T,7) world TCP of the active arm, segment (T,) int8
  (0 pick, 1 place, -1 free-space transit, 2 settle). json extras: active_side, target_category/name, object_label,
  scene, version, source_demo, arm_joint_order, gripper, robot.
- Episodes: 202 steps at 20 Hz (10.1 s). Only one arm moves per episode (active_side); the other holds its ready pose
  (its actions are constant). q_waist is always zeros (welded). Gripper state/action: 0 closed, 1 open.
- ego_rgb 240x320 (head D435 mount, fovy 58 deg); intrinsics in json. No wrist cameras by default (--wrist-cams adds 120x160).
- Indices: scripted 0..99999, MimicGen 100000 + seed (seeds start at 500000, so 600000+); glob episode_*.npz.
- Robot model for F2/ROS: assets/robots/g1_dex1/g1_dex1.xml (joint names `{left,right}_{shoulder_pitch,...,wrist_yaw}_joint`,
  grippers `{side}_dex1_finger_joint_1` (+ coupled _2), joint value = -0.020 + grip * 0.0445).
